# Narrative Coherence Evaluation Rubric

**Based on**: Narrative Flattening paper (arXiv:2605.27878) + RPG Storytelling memory

## Dimensions

### 1. Voice Consistency (Weight: 25%)
- Sentence length variety
- Consistent tone and register
- Avoidance of generic AI phrasing

### 2. Moral Ambiguity (Weight: 20%)
- Presence of ethical tension
- Avoidance of simplistic good/evil framing
- Characters facing meaningful choices

### 3. Delayed Consequence (Weight: 20%)
- Actions have long-term ripple effects
- "Echo across generations" type language
- Non-immediate outcomes

### 4. Folklore Texture (Weight: 15%)
- Mythic, cultural, or archaic language patterns
- Layered meaning and symbolism
- Non-modern narrative flavor

### 5. Narrative Complexity (Weight: 20%)
- Multiple interwoven threads
- Non-linear cause-effect
- Subtext and implication

## Scoring Scale
- 0.0 – 0.4: Poor / Generic
- 0.5 – 0.6: Acceptable
- 0.7 – 0.8: Good
- 0.9 – 1.0: Excellent / Rich

**Threshold for passing (Mythos-style models)**: Overall ≥ 0.62
