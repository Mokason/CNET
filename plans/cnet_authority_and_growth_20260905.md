## Implemented design and scope

The new `cnet_capsule_core` library is an explicit typed serving boundary used
by both a CLI and the real daemon. It imports the existing capsule format;
there is no second artifact format and raw LUTs carry no certification.
Per-request guarded replanning permits coverage expansion with coexisting
immutable versions. Overlapping contradictory contracts fail load.

The learning CLI accepts trusted local user corrections or verified-tool rows,
fits or compiles and certifies a native BTN, and atomically publishes a complete capsule.
The socket regression demonstrates acquisition, two-hop execution, restart,
coverage expansion and preservation of older answers. It is deliberately
not a free-form intent interpreter. The added constrained language adapter
proposes typed intent and rejects recognized ambiguity. The existing scheduler
now consumes bounded, snapshot-bound external-evidence jobs with before/after
regression checks, fair retry, and idempotent publication. Autonomous acquisition
of new teacher evidence is not implemented by this queue.

The legacy learner deployment is a separate acceptance item: its one unguarded
mined unit failed external revalidation. No coverage was fabricated. The user
approved a preserved-base quarantine/restart: the active copy retains 1,095
units, and the excluded unit plus original base remain recoverable. Running
learner health now passes. Maintenance jobs pause/resume that learner to avoid
overwriting external changes with its stale in-memory base. The miner seals
recorded teacher rows and persists their coverage before checkpointing.
