### Quality note

Prompt was **raw token ids**, not a chat-templated string, so degenerate
repetition is expected (not a product chat eval). Integrity signals are
positive: load succeeds after sparse clear, MTK binds hundreds of sites,
generation completes with finite logits.

Gemma-4-12B refused (KV-sharing arch geometry). Eco profile's SPARSE_KV
blocks qwen35 until host clears it and retries (fixed in mtk_host).
