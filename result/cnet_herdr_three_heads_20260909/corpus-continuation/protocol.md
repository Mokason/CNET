# Private confirmation corpus continuation

The continuation repairs an unscored Hermes/Grok draft before admission. This
directory contains metadata only. Corpus requests, labels and review prompts
remain private under `/tmp/cnet-herdr-three-heads-PK7L3c/evaluator/` until the
coordinator freezes the candidate and performs the single authorized evaluation.

The custodian did not inspect CNET parser/source, development examples, or
candidate outputs, and did not execute CNET. The existing `validate_draft.py`
was run as authorized to validate schema, quotas, bounds and exact overlap. It
loads the frozen evaluator validator and reads old collections for exact-text
overlap only; no old corpus texts were inspected or used to generate prompts.
